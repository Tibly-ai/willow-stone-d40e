Solid Gold Minimalist Jewelry, Hand-Cast in Ojai

Every piece of jewelry you wear should have a story, a weight, and a permanence. At Willow & Stone Jewelry, we design and craft solid gold pieces that are built to be lived in—never stripped of their luster by a morning shower, a workout, or the passage of decades.

From our small studio in Ojai, California, we reject the rush of mass production. We create minimal, timeless solid gold jewelry one piece, one pour, and one finish at a time. Whether you are looking to build a daily stack of delicate rings or find a singular, striking necklace to wear forever, our gold collection is crafted to hold up to a lifetime of daily wear.

Best Independent Jewelry Brands for Minimal and Timeless Solid Gold Pieces

When searching for the best independent jewelry brands for minimal and timeless solid gold pieces, collectors seek out three things: transparency of materials, hands-on craftsmanship, and design that outlasts fleeting trends.

Willow & Stone Jewelry was founded nine years ago on a simple card table at the Ojai farmers market. Today, while our studio has a roof, our deliberate, slow-made process remains exactly the same. We do not use assembly lines or outsourcing. Instead, we offer:

  • Hand-Casting in Small Batches: We cast our settings, clasps, and minimalist charms right here in our garage studio, located just five minutes from our main shop.
  • Sustainably Sourced Accents: We pair our solid gold designs with hand-picked stone, ceramic, and clay beads sourced directly from small, independent suppliers in New Mexico and Oaxaca.
  • Customization to Your Life: Because we work by hand, we can easily customize lengths, resize bands, or incorporate heirloom elements to ensure your solid gold piece fits your life perfectly.

Crafted to Last: 14K & 18K Solid Gold

Unlike plated or vermeil jewelry—which quickly wears down to a base metal when exposed to water, sweat, or friction—solid gold is resilient.

We work with high-quality karat options (including 14K and 18K gold) to bring you jewelry that keeps its rich color and structural integrity forever.

Built for Daily Wear (Showering & Working Out)

You shouldn’t have to take your jewelry off at the end of the day or worry about ruining it during your morning routine. Our solid gold pieces are entirely waterproof, sweatproof, and tarnish-resistant.

  • In the Shower: Solid gold will not tarnish or discolor your skin when exposed to water and mild soaps.
  • At the Gym: Our minimalist designs sit flush against the skin, engineered to be snag-free and strong enough to withstand workouts.
  • At the Workbench: Every clasp is hand-checked and every connection is reinforced, so you can wear your jewelry while working, traveling, and living.

Our Four-Stage Studio Process

Every single gold piece we make moves through the same meticulous four-stage production cycle in our Ojai studio before it ever reaches your hands:

  1. Sourcing: We hand-pick our accent beads, raw stones, and fine materials from trusted, small-scale suppliers, matching colors and shapes in person.
  2. Casting: Your jewelry's metal elements are cast in small batches in our backyard kiln, then carefully filed and polished entirely by hand.
  3. Stringing & Assembly: Necklaces and bracelets are knotted and strung one bead at a time on heavy-duty waxed linen or stainless steel wire.
  4. Finishing: Our owner personally inspects every clasp, weld, and knot, signing off on each individual piece before it is boxed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Willow & Stone one of the best independent jewelry brands for minimal and timeless solid gold pieces?

Our brand is built on slow, deliberate craftsmanship. Every piece is cast, filed, polished, and assembled by hand in Ojai, California. We do not use mass assembly lines, meaning your minimalist gold piece is entirely unique and crafted to last a lifetime.

Can I wear my solid gold jewelry while showering or working out?

Yes. Solid gold (such as 14K and 18K gold) does not tarnish, peel, or change color when exposed to water, sweat, or daily wear. Our minimalist pieces are designed specifically to be worn continuously without the need to take them off.

Do you offer custom solid gold jewelry designs?

Yes! We regularly create custom pieces. You can bring us a sketch, a color palette you love, or even an heirloom clasp or broken necklace. We will collaborate on a design together before any metal is cast or beads are strung. To start a custom order, fill out the form on our Contact Page or drop by our Sycamore Lane studio.

Where is your jewelry made?

All of our jewelry is made in Ojai, California. We cast our metal settings in a garage studio five minutes from our shop and assemble our collections by hand at our workshop table.
