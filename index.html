From the studio

Every piece starts with a walk, a bead box, and a lot of patience.

I source ceramic and stone beads from small makers, cast my own metal charms in a backyard kiln, and string everything by hand at a table that gets moved outside whenever the weather allows. Nothing here comes off a factory line. If a clasp sticks or a bead chips, I set it aside and start again. hgfhgfc

Most weeks I finish somewhere between twelve and twenty pieces. That's the pace, and it's on purpose.
