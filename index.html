From the studio

Every piece starts with a walk, a bead box, and a lot of patience.

I source ceramic and stone beads from small makers, cast my own metal charms in a backyard kiln, and string everything by hand at a table that gets moved outside whenever the weather allows. Nothing here comes off a factory line. If a clasp sticks or a bead chips, I set it aside and start again.

Most weeks I finish somewhere between twelve and twenty pieces. That's the pace, and it's on purpose.

What we make

Three ways to wear the studio

Necklaces

Long strands and short chokers, mixing raw stone, ceramic, and brass. No two beading patterns repeat exactly.

Earrings & Bracelets

Cast and hammered by hand in small batches, then paired with beads left over from bigger necklace runs.

Custom Pieces

Bring a broken necklace, an heirloom clasp, or a color you love. We'll sketch something together before any beads get strung.
